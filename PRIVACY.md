# Privacy policy: Relink

Last updated: 2 October 2026

## Your CMS data

Relink runs inside Framer, in the plugin window on your device. It reads and changes your CMS only through Framer's Plugin API, and computes relationships (Find Usage, Cleanup, previews) locally in the plugin. Relink doesn't operate any backend, database or server for CMS data, and never sends your CMS content anywhere. We never receive it.

When you apply a change, it's written to your Framer project through the Plugin API and becomes part of your project, under Framer's privacy policy.

## What is stored on your device

To make Undo possible, Relink keeps an operation history in your browser's local storage, separately for each project. For each operation it stores only the reference fields it changed: item and field identifiers, item and field names, and which items were referenced before and after. It keeps the last 50 operations. You can delete it at any time with **History → Clear History**.

If you have Relink Pro, it also stores your license key, its activation ID and when it was last checked.

It never stores other CMS content, and never stores any payment information.

## License checks (Pro only)

When you activate Relink Pro, and about once a day afterwards, the plugin sends your license key to [Polar](https://polar.sh) to confirm it's valid, together with Relink's public product identifiers and a device label ("Relink for Framer"). Nothing else is sent: no CMS data, no project content, no browsing data.

## No analytics

Relink doesn't collect usage analytics, crash reports or telemetry.

## Payments

Purchases are processed by Polar Software, Inc. as the merchant of record. We never see or store your card details. Polar's own privacy policy applies to your purchase: https://polar.sh/legal/privacy-policy
