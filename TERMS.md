# Terms and refunds: Relink

Last updated: 2 October 2026

## The product

Relink is a plugin for Framer. The Free version is free to use. **Relink Pro** is a one-time purchase of **$19.99 (USD)** that unlocks the Pro features on up to 3 devices. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

## Payment

Payments are processed by Polar Software, Inc., which acts as the merchant of record and handles taxes. Polar's buyer terms also apply to your purchase: https://polar.sh/legal/checkout-buyer-terms

## License

- Your license key is for your own use. Please don't share it publicly.
- You can move your license between devices: open the license details in the plugin and choose **Remove from This Device**, then activate it on the new one.
- A refunded purchase deactivates the license key.

## Refunds

If Relink Pro doesn't work for you, contact us within **14 days** of your purchase for a full refund.

## Your CMS

Relink changes your CMS only when you apply an operation, and only the reference fields shown in its preview. Always review the preview before applying. Relink re-checks items before writing and keeps a history so you can undo, but Undo is best-effort: it restores fields that still hold Relink's change and can't restore CMS items that were deleted. Keep backups of important projects (for example by duplicating the project in Framer). Relink is provided as is, and we can't be responsible for any loss resulting from its use.
